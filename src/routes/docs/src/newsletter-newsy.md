Reaching your audience directly by email is among the most effective ways to
build a loyal, dedicated audience. Yet the conventional way of running a newsletter has grown
unnecessarily clunky, bloated, and costly.

LightCMS takes a modern, developer-friendly approach instead: **Git driven newsletters** powered by [Newsy](https://newsy.lightcms.me/). for further details, check out the [repo](https://github.com/light-cms/newsy).

---

## The Traditional Newsletter Problem

With standard platforms such as Mailchimp, Substack, or Brevo, your workflow usually involves:

1. Registering with an outside service that has inflexible subscription tiers.
2. Juggling scattered mailing lists across third-party dashboards.
3. Copying and pasting your blog post by hand into proprietary rich-text email builders.
4. Coping with bloated tracking scripts, cookies, and privacy compliance obligations.
5. Paying hefty monthly fees as your subscriber base expands.

---

## The Git-Driven Approach: Newsy

The **Newsy** philosophy is straightforward: **Your Git repository serves as the single source of truth for your newsletter.**

[Newsy](https://newsy.lightcms.me) is an open source, automated newsletter service that keeps an eye on your GitHub repository:

Newsy watches repository events and smartly detects newly created content files in your configured repo. It takes care of everything else by itself.
Find out more and explore the [Newsy Website](https://newsy.lightcms.me).
