### Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much CO2 Does a Typical Website Emit?

Estimates vary by method, but a typical page view emits on the average a gram or two grams of CO2; heavy, poorly cached pages emit considerably more. Static, cached pages sit at the low end like 0.002 grams/visit.

### Is a Static Blog Eco Friendly?

Among the greenest options available: cached files served from cdn, no always on database no server render all the time, and content that remains readable and rebuildable for decades.

### What is sustainable web?

A practice of minimizing the energy cost of the web: lean pages, efficient code, renewable hosting, durable content, and measured impact of which static architecture is the foundation.
