## Light CMS

A lightweight, Git-backed content workspace designed for seamless editing,
AI-assisted writing, and ultimate flexibility.

1. Git-Backed Storage: Manage your content with the version control you already have.

2. Modern Editor: A clean, distraction free editing environment built for
   speed, focus, and rich content creation.

3. AI Copilot Integration: A built-in AI assistant to help you draft, refine,
   and brainstorm content directly alongside your work.

4. Multi-Theme Support: First-class light and dark mode implementations to
   seamlessly match your system or workflow preferences.

5. Stateless Architecture: Built with a fully stateless authentication design
   for enhanced performance and simplicity.
