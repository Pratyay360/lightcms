## Overview

**Light CMS** is a lightweight, Git-backed content workspace designed for
seamless editing, AI-assisted writing, and ultimate flexibility.

- 🗂️ **Git-Backed Storage** — Manage your content with the version control you already have.
- ✍️ **Modern Editor** — A clean, distraction-free editing environment built for speed, focus, and rich content creation.
- 🤖 **AI Copilot Integration** — A built-in AI assistant to help you draft, refine, and brainstorm content directly alongside your work.
- 🌗 **Multi-Theme Support** — First-class light and dark mode implementations to seamlessly match your system or workflow preferences.
